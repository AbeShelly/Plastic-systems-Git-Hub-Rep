## CONCLUSIONS (census-followup)
1. 1920: Charles A. Bayhurst NOT FOUND. Not in Mill Creek Twp (all EDs 128-132 scanned on images) and not in the Ancestry 1920 index
   for Pennsylvania under Bayhurst/Bay* (index negative). Brothers found ON IMAGE: Louis [Lewis J.] 25, head, owner, "Tire maker /
   Rubberworks", with Frank 18 "Chauffer / Motor truck" and Rosa 15, at 40 Elliott Ave, Millcreek Twp ED 129 (5 Feb 1920);
   Edward 2? "driver / truck", wife Ruth, 1206 E 22nd St, Erie Ward 5 ED 103 (3 Jan 1920). No motorcycle occupation in the family 1920.
2. 1930->1936 Hammermill: Charles was on the 1930 paid-vacation schedule (Mar 1 1930 issue, 4 days, GENERAL dept) but is NOT among the
   May 1936 ten-year pin recipients, whereas his May 1926 hiring cohort (incl. Walter Trawka, same May 20 start, and Peter Gartner, his
   GENERAL-dept list partner) is. He left Hammermill (or broke continuous service) between Mar 1930 and May 1936. Issues Sep 1930-Nov 1933
   are not digitized, so the exact exit (e.g. May 1931 5-year pin) cannot be checked online. Consistent with (does not prove) the
   Apr 1930 census "Owner / Bicycles / own account".
3. 1940, 302 E 11th St: house number and household number 80 recorded (Sheet 4A line 2, 5 Apr 1940) but NO persons -> no residents
   enumerated at 302 E 11th in April 1940; the census does not record businesses, so whether the shop was still operating is not
   shown. Neighbours: 304 = Crescenzo Cioccio (cf. 1939 directory "304 Cioccio Crescenzo gro") and Ed Preuss.
4. 1940, Lewis Bayhurst (wife Elma), 192[4?] Sassafras St (last digit uncertain), carpenter, house builder, private wage worker, 24 weeks / $720 in 1939.
   Not in the motorcycle trade.
5. No record links Bayhurst to 120 E 11th; operator of the 1935-36 Indian Motorcycle Agency at 120 E 11th still unknown.
