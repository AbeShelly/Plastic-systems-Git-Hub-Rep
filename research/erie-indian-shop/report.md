# The Indian motorcycle shop on "East 10th Street", Erie PA — research report

**Status: FINAL (v3).** Round 1 used 6 research lanes plus a critic. Round 2 added 4 follow-up searches and 3 independent
verifiers that re-fetched every page image and tried to refute each claim. **66 image-checked claims: 0 refuted, 22 reworded.**
Research date: 4 Oct 2026. Run from a cloud container (the NUC was offline): no logged-in browser and no paid sources.

---

## 1. The answer

**The shop:** listed as **"Indian Motorcycle Agency"**, on **East 11th Street** — not East 10th.
**Where:** **120 E 11th St** (Jul 1935 – Jul 1936), then **302 E 11th St**, the corner of German St (Jul 1937 – Aug 1939).
**Who:** **Charles Arthur Bayhurst** (born Erie, 28 Aug 1899 – died 1978) ran the 302 E 11th shop. Nothing names who ran 120 E 11th in 1935–36.

| What | Finding | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| Name | "Indian Motorcycle Agency": Erie's only Indian listing in the phone books 1934–1947 | **VERIFIED on page images**, re-checked by an independent verifier. One source type (phone books: 4 editions, white + yellow pages) |
| 120 E 11th St, phone 69-312 | Jul 1935, Jan 1936, Jul 1936 | **VERIFIED**, 1 source type |
| 302 E 11th St | Jul 1937 phone book: "Indian Motorcycle Agency 302 E 11th st…48-385" **and** "Bayhurst Charles motorcycles 302 E 11th st..48-385". Aug 1939 city directory: "Bayhurst Chas A (Theresa I) motor-cycles 302 E 11th h 847 E 7th" | **CONFIRMED**: 2 independent publishers (Pennsylvania Telephone Corp. 1937; Atkinson Erie Directory 1939), both seen on page images |
| Owner | Charles A. Bayhurst ran a motorcycle business at 302 E 11th (1937, 1939). The 1940 census lists him as "mechanic / motorcycle repair shop / OA" (own account) | **CONFIRMED** (phone book + directory + census, all images) |
| Indian franchise ↔ Bayhurst | The Indian agency and Bayhurst share the same address and phone in Jul 1937 | **Strong inference**. The 1939 directory says "motorcycles", with no brand |
| Operator 1935–36 at 120 E 11th | **Not named in any source** (phone, census, directory, trade press, OCR searches) | OPEN |
| "East 10th Street" | **No motorcycle shop with an East 10th address or entrance, 1911–1960** | See §2 |

## 2. East 10th Street: what the evidence says

**Not found at any date.** What was checked:
- **Phone books 1911–1960**: the Motorcycles heading read on page images in 23 Erie editions (1934–1955), and the full text of every filmed edition 1911–1960 searched. No E 10th motorcycle or bicycle business.
- **Fire-insurance (Sanborn) maps 1921 and 1950**: every E 10th frontage, 0–460 blocks, both sides, at full resolution. No "MOTOR CYCLE", "CYCLE" or "BICYCLE" label on E 10th.
  ([1950 sheet 59](evidence/1950-08_sanborn-erie-v1-sheet59_loc-g3824em_g07660195001_114-e11th-motor-cycle-shop.png))
- **c.1929 Nirenstein business map** of downtown Erie: no cycle business on E 10th 1–133 or E 11th 1–130.
  ([crop](evidence/1929c_nirenstein-business-real-estate-erie_e11th-102-130-e10th-107-133-french-holland-block_buick-garage-117-e10th-no-cycle-shop_composite.png))
- **1939 city directory**: the "Motorcycle Dealers" heading lists exactly two dealers, both on E 11th ([p.1247](evidence/1939-08_atkinson-erie-directory_p1247_classified-motorcycle-dealers-bayhurst-302-kuivinen-114-e11th_gbsnippet-x3.png)).
  The only E 10th cycle item is a **Goodyear tire store at 163 E 10th that also sold bicycles** ([crop](evidence/1939-08_atkinson-erie-directory_p347-p1216_goodyear-service-stores-163-e10th-radios-and-bicycles_gbsnippets_composite.png)).

**Most likely explanation:** a one-block slip. In the 1930s East 10th was Erie's car row (Buick dealer, garages, tire shops).
The Indian agency (120 E 11th) and the Harley-Davidson shop (114 E 11th) stood **back-to-back with that row on the same city block**.
On the 1950 map the 11th Street buildings end at the mid-block line, and the 117–129 E 10th taxi garage runs back to it.
**No building runs through, and no opening is drawn** (Sanborn maps don't always show ordinary doors).

**Still open:** the Erie Indian dealer for **1923–1934** is unknown, so a 1920s East 10th shop can't be ruled out completely.
Every free source has been tried: no 1923–40 US motorcycle trade journal is on archive.org, and the only one on Google Books
(American Motorcyclist & Bicyclist, 1928; 29 in-volume searches) names no Erie Indian dealer. It needs the 1920s city
directories (manual step 1).

**One later East 10th motorcycle link** (outside the period, snippet only): a motorcycle **club**, the Erie Wolverine MC, at
2030 E 10th St in 1964–65 (American Motorcyclist listings). It was a club, not a shop.

## 5. The owner: Charles Arthur Bayhurst (1899–1978)

- **Born** 28 Aug 1899 in Erie, son of Louis (b. France; farmer, Mill Creek Twp; d. 1918) and Louise (b. Germany; d. 1916). Brothers Lewis J., Edward L. and Frank W.; sister Rosa.
- **1919:** at 19 he **won the 5-mile motorcycle race** at the Erie Cycle Dealers' Association meet, Exposition Park, 4 July 1919.
- **1920:** not found in the 1920 census (every Mill Creek sheet checked). His brothers were a rubber-works tire maker and truck drivers.
- **About 1922–23:** married Theresa I. Murphy (1901–1983). They lived with her parents at **847 E 7th St** from 1930 to at least 1950.
- **1926–1930:** a "Charles Bayhurst" worked at Hammermill Paper and was gone by 1936 (name match only).
- **1930:** the census reads "Owner / Bicycle[s]", own account.
- **1937–1939:** **motorcycles, 302 E 11th St**: the Indian agency's address and phone in 1937. **1940:** motorcycle repair, own account.
- **1942:** Erie Forge and Steel. **1950:** forge-shop floor hand.
- **Died** 19 Nov 1978 (transcription). **Obituary not yet found** (manual step 4).

## 7. Everything else Indian in Erie

- **Today:** Indian Motorcycle of Erie (Off-Road Express). An Indian Riders Group chapter, "Great Lake Gypsies Chapter 1901", started in 2014 (web search summaries).

## 9. Corrections made by the independent verifiers

The verifiers re-fetched every page image. **0 of 66 claims were refuted** (27 phone-book, 11 people, 28 trade-press/history/maps). 22 were reworded:
- "No Indian dealer" in a year → **"no Indian listed under Motorcycles"** (a missing listing ≠ a closed shop).
- "Bayhurst operated the Indian agency" → **"same address and phone as the agency"** (inference, not printed).
- "Every edition" → **"every LOC-filmed edition"**. The reels lack Jan 1937, Jan 1938, Jul 1939 and other issues.
- The 1942 draft card is the **3rd** registration, not the "old man's" 4th.
- The 1930 census link is [page/n802](https://archive.org/details/fifteenthcensus00reel2035/page/n802), not n803. The dwelling number is 309.
- The 1940 census "40" is in the hours-worked column while the same row says "seeking work", so no shop-closing date can be drawn from it.
- Hoffman was at 405 W 8th by July 1945.
- The "Morehouse Garage, 117 E 10th" (1946) is **not** the Moorhouse Motor Co. of 118 E 11th (different spelling and phone).
- My first reading of the 1920 race snippet was wrong: the "Indian Motorcycle Club" in that sanction list is Hammond, Ind., not Erie.
- Beckman's club presidency is "the past three years" as of Dec 1922 (c.1920–22). Schroeder was "installed in the service department", not put in charge of it.
- The Harley-Davidson Enthusiast with Mr. Furlong is No. 64, September 1923 (the trip was June 1923).
- Three archive.org links opened one page off (some scanned leaves are hidden in the viewer). They are fixed here: 1912 show → /page/n1283, 1909 meet → /page/n1006, Western Massachusetts → /page/799.
- 1950 Sanborn: "no building **labelled** as a motorcycle shop on E 10th". Several stores carry no trade label, and a 1950 map says nothing about 1920s–30s tenants.

Several evidence crops are **stitched composites** (a page-header strip placed above the listing). Each part is unaltered.
