# The Indian motorcycle shop on "East 10th Street", Erie PA — research report

**Status: INTERIM (round 1 of 2).** Round 2 (independent re-verification of every image-checked claim, plus four
follow-up searches) is still running. Anything it changes will be marked in the final version.
Research date: 4 Oct 2026. Run from a cloud container (the NUC was offline), so no logged-in browser was used.

## 1. The answer

**Name:** listed as **"Indian Motorcycle Agency"** in the Erie phone books.
**Owner:** **Charles Arthur Bayhurst** (born 28 Aug 1899 in Erie, died 1978) ran it by July 1937. Nothing names the operator for 1935–36.
**Address:** **East 11th Street**, not East 10th: first **120 E 11th St**, then **302 E 11th St** (the corner of German St).
**Years:** listed July 1935 → July 1937. Bayhurst was still listed as a motorcycle dealer at 302 E 11th in Aug 1939.

|---|---|---|

| Who he was | Charles Arthur Bayhurst of 847 E 7th St: the draft card, 1940 census, 1943 phone book and 1939 directory all tie the name, address and trade together | **CONFIRMED** (draft card + census, both images) |
| Operator 1935–36 (120 E 11th) | **Not named in any source found.** Probably Bayhurst, but unproven | OPEN |
| "East 10th Street" | **No motorcycle shop with an East 10th address found, 1908–1947.** But the 11th St shops were on the same city block as the E 10th car row (see §2) | Not supported (open for 1923–34) |

What it sold: **Indian motorcycles** (the listing name). The 1940 census calls his trade "motorcycle repair shop". The
1930 census most likely says he owned a bicycle business. No ad, brand list or photo of the shop was found.

## 2. East 10th Street — is your memory wrong?

Short version: **probably off by one block, not wrong about the place.**

- Every Erie Indian dealer address found on a printed page is somewhere else:
  1721 Sassafras (LeJeal, about 1908–22) → 1807 Parade (Beckman, 1922) → **120 E 11th** (1935–36) → **302 E 11th** (1937–39).
- The Motorcycles heading of all 16 Erie phone books, 1934–1947, was read on the images. It never lists an E 10th shop.
- A search of all phone-book text 1911–1947 found no motorcycle or bicycle business on E 10th (text search, so a
  garbled line could be missed).
- **The best explanation:** East 10th was Erie's car row in the 1930s (Buick dealer, Goodyear, tire shops, garages — per phone-book text).
  The 1935–36 Indian agency at 120 E 11th and the Harley-Davidson shop at 114 E 11th stood on the north side of
  E 11th, **back-to-back with the E 10th garages on the same block**. On the 1950 Sanborn map, the 118–120 E 11th
  building runs back toward the 117–129 E 10th taxi garage. Someone who knew the shops could easily remember them as
  "on Tenth". *Round 2 is checking whether these buildings ran through to E 10th or had an entrance there.*
- One minor alternative: in 1939 a **Goodyear Service Store at 163 E 10th** also sold bicycles (directory snippet only).

Map evidence: [1950 Sanborn, 100 block E 11th](evidence/1950-08_sanborn-erie-v1-sheet59_loc-g3824em_g07660195001_114-e11th-motor-cycle-shop.png)
(114 = "MOTOR CYCLE SHOP", 118–120 = "AUTO SALES & SERV."), [1921 vs 1950, 302 E 11th corner](evidence/1921-1950_sanborn-erie-v1-sheet61_302-e11th-german-corner.png).

## 4. The owner: Charles Arthur Bayhurst (1899–1978)

- **Born** 28 Aug 1899 in Erie ([draft card](evidence/1942-02-16_nara-wwii-draft-card-pa_44033_05_00028-01413_charles-arthur-bayhurst-front.png)).
  His parents were Louis (born France, about 1863; farmer; died 1918) and Louise (born Germany, about 1862; died 1916).
  His brothers were Lewis J. (1894–1948), Edward Leo (born 1898) and Frank William (born 1901). He had a sister, Rosa ([1910 census](evidence/1910-05-04_census-millcreek-ed124-sheet8B_T624-1343-img0556_bayhurst-family.png)).
- **Married** Theresa I. Murphy (1901–1983). They lived with her parents at **847 East 7th St** from at least 1930 to 1950. Their son Charles William was born in 1927 (index only).
- **Cycle trade:** in Jul 1919 a "Charles Bayhurst" won a motorcycle race at an Erie dealers' event (snippet). The 1930
  census says "Owner / Bicycles". He ran the Indian agency's premises and phone in 1937 (image), was still listed under "motorcycles" in 1939 (snippet), and worked his own motorcycle repair shop in 1939–40 (census, image).
- **Later:** worker at Erie Forge and Steel (1942 draft card; 1950 census).
- **Died** 19 Nov 1978. Buried in Erie Cemetery next to Theresa (transcription). **No obituary found yet** — see the manual steps.
- **Conflict, kept open:** Hammermill Paper's employee magazine lists a "Charles Bayhurst" from May 1926 to Mar 1930.
  The 1930 census (Apr 1930) says "Owner, Bicycles". Both are primary, and the Hammermill match is by name only. Round 2 is checking whether he left the mill in 1930.
